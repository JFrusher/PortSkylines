# M6 playtest: scale, rail, berths and new cargo

**Date:** 26 Oct 2026. **Build:** `main` with the M6 notes branch
(`claude/m6-notes`). **How:** scripted, not a public build: the real scene
(`main.tscn`) under `xvfb-run` with the Compatibility renderer at 1280×720,
driven by the commands the toolbar and Inspect send, with screenshots
checked by eye. The public playtest build (step 21) still needs a person to
play it.

## What was played

| Area | What happened |
|---|---|
| Guided start | A feeder berth clicked onto the coast, the highway road joining its road in the middle, gate, yard block, a crane on a spot, tractors. Three lines offered within the hour and the first feeder was worked inside its window (`test_guide`). |
| Berths | Feeder, Panamax and Neopanamax berths sit flush on the water with their road behind; berths end to end share road ends; crane spots show as yellow stripes and the crane tool snaps to them. |
| Rail | The Mega port's two RMG rail blocks take trains every half hour; trains book the level crossings and lorries wait for them. With 30% by rail the gate queue fell from 8.5 to 6.3 lorries. |
| Reefers | The Mega port plugs 535 reefers into its reefer blocks; the Quay & Power panel shows plugs, power now and the day's peak. |
| Neopanamax | Locked until milestone 4 (1,500 TEU) with the wide STS crane; the Cranes row shows it greyed. |
| Week by berth | The Mega port's seven daily Panamax calls fill berth 1 back to back with no clash. |
| Money per move | A three-day starter port: $154.72 a move in, $112.47 net, per line and per terminal. |
| True scale | Deck boxes match the lorries' boxes and the 4 m lanes; cranes straddle the apron on 30.5 m rails. |

## Found and fixed

1. **Roads didn't centre under the mouse**, and the crane delivery day kept
   the first offers away for a day (hotfix #19).
2. **A line could roll a ship class the port couldn't take and offer
   nothing.** Lines now pick among the classes the port can take (#21).
3. **The Finance panel crashed** on a typed array the tests never opened;
   caught by the screenshot (#22).
4. **The soak test never saw rail.** It now builds an RMG block and track;
   its machine-job checks assumed every job had a vehicle, and train jobs
   don't.

## Open

1. **Reefer fines hit new ports hard.** A starter port with no reefer block
   paid $13,200 in fines on a day its moves earned $40,820: 10% of boxes are
   reefers, fined $400 each against a $60 premium, and the guide never asks
   for a reefer block. Lock reefers to a milestone, add a guide step, or
   lower the fine: a design call.
2. **The endgame bench fails 60 fps on the CPU** (see `docs/perf.md`, M6
   close); the target PC is unmeasured and step 10 is undecided.
3. **The Mega port has no dangerous-goods block**, so its dangerous imports
   are shut out (the event says so).
4. **Yard machines are drawn at the old box height**, taller than the
   true-scale boxes.
