# M8 playtest: the whole port

**Date:** 1 Nov 2026. **Build:** branch `claude/m8-whole-port`. **How:**
scripted, not a public build: the tests and soak, the endgame bench, and the
real scene (`main.tscn`) under `xvfb-run` at 1280×720, checked by screenshot.
The public playtest build still needs a person to play it; the list at the
end is what they should try.

## What was checked

| Area | How |
|---|---|
| Terminals as units | Two terminals keep their own ledger sections and KPIs; a ship signed for one terminal's berth never takes the other's (`test_terminals`). The Port panel lists them. |
| A natural coastline | The Estuary: curved coast, two islands, a river in the north, water deepening offshore (`test_coast`, screenshots of the 3D view and the map). |
| Quays on a curve | A berth faces the nearest water and builds one straight quay at the most seaward land, filling the water behind it at a price; the fill stays when the apron goes (`test_coast`). |
| Ship lanes and depth | Lanes on open water only, straight or 45°; ships use only lanes and berth pockets as deep as their draft, and go round by a dredged lane (`test_ship_lanes`). |
| Anchorage | Overbooking fills the anchorage one spot per ship; full, ships wait at sea and go elsewhere; lateness counts the time at anchor (`test_anchorage`). |
| Passage, pilots, tugs | Two ships never sail a narrow lane at once; one pilot serialises Panamax arrivals; a nearer tug depot berths sooner (`test_marine`). |
| Exit criterion | Through one long narrow lane the queue at anchor grows; widening it or hiring pilots shrinks it, and without lane bookings the test fails (`test_channel`). |
| Fees | Port dues per call and pilotage per piloted passage, in their own ledger lines (`test_channel`). |
| ULCV | Locked until milestone 5; only the mega STS reaches across; light-loaded at 15 m with 60% of its moves, full at 16 m, none at 14 m (`test_ulcv`). |
| Between terminals | An import landed at one terminal reaches the other's export block, the move charged to the first (`test_transfers`). |
| Progression | A milestone stays locked at enough TEU with too little reputation and stays reached once reached; an objective pays once; every unlock is in `docs/progression.md` (`test_progression`). |
| Breakwaters and islands | No lane crosses either (`test_ship_lanes`). |
| Generated coasts | Same seed, same map; twenty seeds playable (`test_map_gen`, map screenshots). |
| Crane models | Before and after in `docs/media/m8_cranes_*.png`; 113 fewer draw calls (`docs/perf.md`). |

## Found and fixed

1. **Yard use counted boxes, not TEU**: a slot holds two 20 ft boxes, so a full block could read 200% (120% seen).
2. **A new estuary game could never get a ship**: the shore was 4–6 m deep, feeders need 10 m and dredging unlocks at milestone 3. The shelf now starts at 10.5 m, as the old 11 m channel did.
3. **The Water tab pushed the toolbar off a 1280 px screen**: its buttons are narrower.
4. **A leaving ship held its berth until it was off the map**, so berths, not the channel, held ships up; a berth now frees once its ship is out of the pocket.
5. **The port map kept the last game's ground** after a new game or a load (its cache keys were version numbers that start again with every sim).
6. **The bench and debug mega port had no ship lanes** after step D, so their Panamaxes couldn't berth.

## Open

1. **Balance of the new costs is unmeasured**: lanes, dredging, pilots, tugs, anchorages, port dues and objective rewards are first guesses in the data; a played game should check the money curve to milestone 3 and 4.
2. **Tugs are drawn only as their depot**; ships sail in without visible tugs or pilot boats.
3. **The early game asks for a lane** as well as a berth (a new guide step); a playtester should say whether that's clear.

## For the public build

1. Start on the Estuary: does the guide's lane step make sense, and is the berth click on a curved coast clear?
2. Reach Gateway and bring in a Panamax: is it clear why it waits (pilot, tugs, depth)?
3. Build an anchorage, then overbook: do waiting ships read on the water?
4. Paint a second terminal, sign a line at both, and watch boxes move between them.
5. Start "New game, new coast" a few times: are the coasts varied and fair?
