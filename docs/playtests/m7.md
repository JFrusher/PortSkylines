# M7 playtest: traffic, junctions and painted terminals

**Date:** 31 Oct 2026. **Build:** branch `claude/m7-routing`. **How:**
scripted, not a public build: tests, the soak and the endgame bench, plus
the real scene (`main.tscn`) under `xvfb-run` at 1280×720 for the map,
checked by screenshot. The public playtest build still needs a person to
play it; the checklist below is what they should try.

## What was checked

| Area | How |
|---|---|
| Live delays | Each lane cell's delay settles every 10 s; the Traffic view shows the jammed cells (`test_lane_traffic`). |
| Cost routing | Lorries route round a jammed road when a free one exists, and keep out of parks and handover slots they are not using (`test_routing`, `test_parking`). |
| Turn lanes | Left from the leftmost lane, right from the rightmost, straight from the middle; no lane changes inside a junction (`test_junctions`). |
| Signals | Opposite arms share a phase; a phase changes only once its green is over and the box is empty; timings edit from Inspect (`test_signals`). |
| Priority signs | Give way and stop hold the minor arm while the major arm is busy (`test_signals`). |
| Painted terminals | Brush, erase and fill on the map; an area joins the terminal that holds most of its cells; unpainted areas stand idle and say why (`test_terminals`). |
| Saves | v24 saves and loads; older saves are refused with a message (`test_save`). |
| Soak | Random commands for a game week with the port working; every route steps cell to cell (`test_soak`). |

## Found and fixed

1. **Lorries gridlocked parks** by cutting through them and their spaces.
   Area and slot cells are now closed to a search unless it starts or ends
   there.
2. **A yard block took more boxes than it held** ("box doesn't fit on stack
   −1"): lorries under the crane were not counted. Fixed on its own commit.
3. **Painting lagged** (101 ms a stroke step) while the map image was
   rebuilt; it now repaints only the cells that change (20 steps in 9 ms).

## For the public build

1. Paint a terminal on an empty site, then build into it: does the guide
   make the paint step clear?
2. Repaint half a yard into a second terminal while lorries work it.
3. Jam the gate road and watch whether lorries take a second road.
4. Put signals on a busy junction, then stop signs; edit the green times.
5. Load a save from before M7: is the refusal clear enough?
