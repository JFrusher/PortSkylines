# M9 playtest: barge and feeder

**Date:** 1 Nov 2026. **Build:** branch `claude/m9-barge`. **How:**
scripted, not a public build. Checked through the tests and the soak, the
showcase port (debug menu → *Showcase port*), and the real scene
(`main.tscn`) under `xvfb-run` at 1280×720, by screenshot. The public
playtest build still needs a person to play it; the list at the end is
what they should try.

## What was checked

| Area | How |
|---|---|
| Transhipment | About the line's share of a big ship's boxes go on with its next feeder. None leaves by lorry, the feeder loads them first, and a transhipment move pays the price times the factor (`test_transhipment`). |
| Transhipment blocks | Boxes stack in the transhipment block nearest the onward berth, never in the import block. The role waits for milestone 3 (`test_transhipment`). |
| Missed connections | A box not loaded in its window is fined once per window and rolls on, and its line thinks less of the port (`test_transhipment`). |
| Barges up the river | A barge comes only by the river's inland end, a feeder only from the sea, and only barges sail a river channel. Verified to fail with the rule off (`test_barges`). |
| Barge berths | A barge berth takes only barges, and a barge only a barge berth (`test_barges`). |
| Barge boxes | Imports for places up the river are booked to barge only while barges call. No lorry takes them, a barge loads exactly them, and what it lands goes out as exports (`test_barges`). |
| Three-way split | At the maximum barge discount, more boxes go by barge and fewer by both rail and road (`test_barges`). The board shows each line's split (screenshot). |
| Straddle carriers | A straddle block works a feeder with no tractors: 91 minutes with five carriers a block, 314 with one (`test_straddle`). |
| Soak | Half a game day of the showcase port, with M9's features, under every soak invariant. Two new ones: no lorry takes a box bound for a ship or barge, and straddle carriers are counted (`test_soak`). |
| Saves | v34–v37 migrate every older save, each with its fixture. |

## Found and fixed

1. **A berth on a curving shore could be approved and then fail to build.** Its road runs one cell past its apron, and the fill didn't reach that cell. Players could have hit this.
2. **Barge booking happened where no barge ever calls**, so imports waited forever. Booking also sat ahead of reefer placement, leaving reefers unplugged. Now booking needs a barge service, and it no longer changes where a box is stacked.
3. **Boxes carried away by barge weren't counted as delivered**, and empties never came back for them.
4. **The barge row widened the berth board past the screen**, pushing its Close button and Friday off it.
5. **Cranes looked for their next box before checking a vehicle was free**, which took the showcase from 3.4 ms to 12.9 ms a tick.
6. **The North Terminal had no dangerous-goods block** in the showcase, so it turned DG boxes away.
7. **The soak's "every box is somewhere" check missed boxes between terminals** (M8's transfers). The showcase was the first soaked port with two terminals.

## Open

1. **Straddle blocks cost the showcase about 1.6 ms a tick** (`docs/perf.md`): cranes look for a box every tick while carriers are free.
2. **Balance is unmeasured:** barge services, the barge discount, missed-connection fines and reputation, and straddle carrier counts are first guesses in the data.
3. **The straddle carrier model hasn't been seen up close.** The camera's nearest zoom shows it too small to judge.
4. **Discharge sends boxes to a terminal's first import block with room, in build order**, not the nearest one. A port with a straddle block and RTG blocks fills them in the order they were built.

## For the public build

1. Build a barge berth on the river and draw a river channel to it. Is it clear that barges come down the river and big ships can't?
2. Raise the barge discount on the berth board. Do the road, rail and barge shares make sense, and does the gate queue fall?
3. Overbook a feeder line. Do missed connections read clearly, and does the fine feel fair?
4. Swap a block to straddle carriers. Is it clear that it needs no tractors?
