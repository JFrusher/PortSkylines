# M11 playtest: harbour life

**Date:** 16 Nov 2026. **Build:** branch `claude/harbour-life` (PR #43). **How:**
scripted, not a public build. Checked through the tests and two soaks
(`test_soak`, `test_soak_m11`), the showcase port (debug menu → *Showcase
port*), long probes of the showcase under steady demand, and the real scene
(`main.tscn`) under `xvfb-run` at 1920×1080, by screenshot. The public
playtest build still needs a person to play it; the list at the end is what
they should try.

## Found and fixed

1. **A berth whose back road met another road at its middle got no driveways.** The lane beside its buffer's middle was a junction, so its traffic direction couldn't be read and the whole area was skipped. Its tractors could be placed under its cranes but never route back, so the port gridlocked within a game day. Four of the showcase's five berths were built this way. Any player building a T-junction at a berth would have hit it. It predates M11 (checked against commit 8eaa975).
2. **New games drew no coast**: the incremental coast redraw (needed for 4.8 km sites) never drew a coast with nothing filled yet.
3. **The test ports and the showcase sat in the sea** on the doubled map; they keep a 2.4 km site now, and saves keep the site they were made on.
4. **The town's noise and light were weighed so that a big port pinned its mood at zero**; re-weighed from the showcase's sources.
5. **The thumbnail tool hung on a refused command** and **aimed its first camera before it was in the tree**.

## Open

1. **The showcase's highway road chokes on lorries.** After the driveway fix the port keeps working (day 0: 1,937 moves), but from about game hour 6 the one road in from the highway fills: 150 to 220 vehicles stopped there, lorries queueing for the gates and truck park, about 850 giving up over 2.5 days, and daily moves falling (870, then 393). The gates are not the limit (about 1,900 lorries an hour). Two theories were tested and ruled out: the stocked imports all coming due at once (spreading them over three days only delayed the choke), and U-turns at the site edge blocking it (an off-map interchange took the U-turners off the road but the choke stayed; reverted). The evidence points at lorries arriving faster than the yards take them on a one-road layout. Next: measure yard handovers against lorry arrivals per block, and try a second road in.
2. **Scenario targets are set from measured rates, not played wins.** A scripted win takes about 40 minutes of sim per scenario; the targets (12,000 to 15,000 moves, 20 on-time calls) come from a mature port's ~1,900 moves on a good day, less a few days' build-up. A person should play each.
3. **AGVs aren't in the showcase**: an AGV depot turns its whole terminal over to AGVs, which needs transfer blocks for lorries. A showcase terminal would need redesigning.
4. **`test_customs` runs about 5 minutes on a slow machine** (CI passes it, faster).
